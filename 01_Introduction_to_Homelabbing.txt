Introduction to Homelabbing

Homelabbing is the practice of building and maintaining a personal IT
environment at home for learning, experimentation, and self-hosting. A
homelab can range from a single Raspberry Pi to a full rack of
enterprise servers.

Common Goals: - Learn networking, virtualization, and system
administration - Practice cybersecurity skills - Host personal services
(file storage, media streaming, automation) - Prepare for IT
certifications

Typical Hardware: - Old desktops or servers - Single-board computers -
Managed switches - Consumer or prosumer routers - Network-attached
storage (NAS)

Homelabbing encourages hands-on learning and experimentation without
risking production systems.
